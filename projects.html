Our Projects

Welcome to Next Tokyo.

Sicarius is a cyberpunk action-RPG set in a world where megacorporations rule from above, the streets belong to gangs and mercenaries, and humanity has blurred the line between flesh and machine. Step into the role of Ghost, a cybernetic assassin caught between forgotten conspiracies, corporate warfare, ancient powers, and the ghosts of his own past. Explore a dangerous open world, take contracts, uncover secrets, upgrade your cybernetics, and decide how far you're willing to go to survive.

Built with a passion for cyberpunk, RPGs, Japanese-inspired storytelling, and the freedom to forge your own path, Sicarius is more than a game—it's a world we're building together. From the neon streets of Next Tokyo to the darkest corners of the NeuroNET, every system, faction, mission, and story is being designed to create a living world worth getting lost in.
